Boom Cannon: Online Battles Support

For technical support, privacy requests, safety reports, or moderation appeals, email bittechs.dev@gmail.com.

Boom Cannon support will never ask for your password, authentication token, or complete device identifier.

Technical support

Include a short description, device model, Android version, Boom Cannon version, and any displayed error. Do not include secrets.

Report or block a public username

From a supported leaderboard, daily board, or room player row, open the player's action menu and choose Report or Block.

Delete your account

Use Account > Delete Account in Boom Cannon, or follow the external deletion instructions.

Age eligibility

Boom Cannon is intended only for users aged 13 or older. The restricted screen allows an incorrectly entered birth year to be corrected.
