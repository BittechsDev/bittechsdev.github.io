PixelFuse: Online Bomber Game Support

For technical support, privacy requests, safety reports, or moderation appeals, email bittechs.dev@gmail.com.

PixelFuse support will never ask for your password, authentication token, or complete device identifier.

Technical support

Include a short description of the problem, the device model, operating system version, PixelFuse version, and any error message shown. Do not include a password or other sensitive information.

Report or block a public username

On a supported leaderboard, daily board, or friend-room player row, open the player's action menu and choose Report or Block. You can also email the support address for a safety concern or moderation appeal.

Delete your account

In PixelFuse, open the Account screen, select Delete Account, and complete the confirmation.

To request deletion without using the App, email bittechs.dev@gmail.com with the subject PixelFuse Account Deletion Request. Include your public username, but never include your password. We may request reasonable information to verify account ownership.

Read the complete account and data deletion information .

Age eligibility

PixelFuse is intended only for users aged 13 or older. If the wrong birth year was entered, use Correct birth year on the restricted screen. Contact support if further assistance is needed.
